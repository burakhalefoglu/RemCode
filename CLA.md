## Why this exists

RemLinkAgent is dual-licensed by directory: the core under AGPL-3.0-or-later, the mobile client under Apache-2.0 ([ADR-002](docs/decisions.md#adr-002--split-licensing-agpl-core-apache-20-mobile)). The project also wants to keep open the possibility of offering a separate commercial licence to organisations that cannot adopt AGPL.

Both require someone who holds the rights to relicense. Once a contribution lands without that permission, it can never be obtained at reasonable cost — which is why this is asked for up front rather than later. The full reasoning, including the earlier "no CLA" position this replaces, is in [ADR-003](docs/decisions.md#adr-003--cla-required).

**What this is not:** a copyright assignment. You keep ownership of everything you write.
